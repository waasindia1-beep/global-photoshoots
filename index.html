Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Global Photoshoots.

Can AI photoshoots accurately replicate my exact physical product?

Yes! For custom garments, watches, or beauty packaging, we train lightweight LoRA adapter models directly on photos of your physical product. This guarantees 100% preservation of logos, fabric weave, seam stitching, and labels without hallucinated flaws.

Do I get full commercial copyright ownership?

Absolutely. Every delivered image and video comes with a worldwide, perpetual commercial usage buyout. You are free to publish them on billboards, TV commercials, Amazon product listings, social ads, and print lookbooks with zero recurring model royalties.

How fast will I receive my campaign files?

Standard campaign deliveries are completed within 48 to 72 hours. If you have an urgent product launch or flash sale, our Rush Delivery option can deliver production-ready 8K master renders within 24 hours.

Can you generate cinematic AI video reels for Instagram & TikTok?

Yes! We specialize in 4K 60FPS cinematic AI video reels, camera dollies, slow-motion fabric flows, and model runway clips. These are optimized for 9:16 vertical reels and standard 16:9 widescreen commercial displays.

How do we initiate a project and handle payments?

Simply click any WhatsApp or Instagram DM button to message Sudhir directly. We review your scope, provide sample preview renders, and invoice via Stripe, Wise, UPI, or PayPal with milestone payments.

# Design brief: Global Photoshoots social assets

Production brief for the visual assets of Global Photoshoots, a commercial product photography and short product video studio in New Delhi. Owner: Sudhir Kumar Solanki. Instagram: @global.photoshoots.

Audience: D2C brand founders and Amazon, Flipkart and Meesho sellers, on a phone, in India. They scroll fast. Legibility on a small screen matters more than polish. The same assets serve the Facebook page, so the profile, the grid and the page read as one studio.

This is a production instruction. Build each asset to the spec stated, then run the pre-publish checklist at the end before anything goes live.

## Brand colours

Use these exactly so social matches the website:

| Use | Hex |
|---|---|
| Deep background | #07090e |
| Card / raised surface | #0f131f |
| Cyan accent | #38bdf8 |
| Indigo accent | #6366f1 |
| Violet accent | #8b5cf6 |
| Text | white |

Rules:

- White text sits on #07090e or #0f131f only.
- Accents mark one element per frame: a mark, a rule, a price. Never accent-coloured body text.
- No invented tints, gradients or extra colours.

## 1. Profile photo

Concept: a monogram or a single bold mark. Instagram shows the profile photo at roughly 40 px in the feed, inside a circle. It has to read as a shape, not a logo with small text.

Spec:

- Export square, 320 x 320 px. If you want headroom for later crops, use 640 x 640 px; Instagram downsizes either.
- One mark only: the letters GP in a single heavy weight, or one bold geometric shape such as a square frame with a cyan corner. Pick one and use it on every asset.
- White mark on #07090e, or a #38bdf8 mark on #07090e. No gradient, no tagline, no small text.

Safe area: Instagram crops the photo into a circle close to the frame edge. Keep the whole mark inside the central 70 percent of the square, a circle of about 224 px at 320 px or 448 px at 640 px. Nothing important within 15 percent of any edge.

Test: shrink the export to 40 px. If you cannot tell what it is, simplify it until you can.

## 2. Instagram cover / featured image

This image sits at the top of the profile grid and does one job: say what he does and make someone want to message.

Exact text, three lines, large:

Product photography
New Delhi
WhatsApp 75575 75514

Spec: 1080 x 1350 px (4:5, matches the feed). Headline on a #0f131f card over a #07090e background. The phone number in cyan #38bdf8. Nothing else on the image. No camera or studio stock imagery, and no sample copy that implies a conventional shoot, because the work is built around client product photos.

## 3. Highlight cover set

Highlights needed, in this order: How It Works, Pricing, Amazon and Flipkart Specs, Before / After, BTS, and Reviews. Add Reviews last and only once there is content behind it, per SETUP.md.

Cover concept: one idea per cover. A single bold word on #0f131f, white, with one accent colour at most. No photos on covers, no icons that need explaining.

| Cover | Word on the cover | Accent for the underline rule |
|---|---|---|
| How It Works | HOW IT WORKS | cyan #38bdf8 |
| Pricing | PRICING | indigo #6366f1 |
| Amazon and Flipkart Specs | SPECS | cyan #38bdf8 |
| Before / After | BEFORE AFTER | violet #8b5cf6 |
| BTS | BTS | indigo #6366f1 |
| Reviews | REVIEWS | violet #8b5cf6 |

How to keep the set uniform, so it does not look assembled from whatever was to hand:

- One master file at 1080 x 1080 px. Every cover is exported from that master at 1080 x 1080 px.
- Same background (#0f131f), same type family, same weight, same word position (centred), same margin (15 percent on every side).
- Same accent treatment: one 8 px rule under the word, identical on every cover.
- Build all six covers in one sitting, before uploading any of them. If a cover looks different from the others, it was not built from the master.

Readability test: shrink each cover to 64 px. If the word is still readable, it passes.

## 4. Reusable post templates

Two working templates. Everything else on the grid is a variant of one of these two.

### Template A: static and carousel

Spec: 4:5, 1350 x 1688 px.

Layout zones, measured from the top-left corner:

- Headline zone: y 180 to 480 px, centred, maximum two lines, inside the side margins (x 100 to 1250 px).
- Supporting line: y 500 to 580 px, one line, directly under the headline.
- Image zone: y 640 to 1420 px. The product image or before-and-after pair sits here.
- Footer strip: y 1488 to 1688 px, a full-width #0f131f band. In this order, on one line or two stacked lines: price, marketplaces (Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho), city (New Delhi), WhatsApp +91 75575 75514.
- Logo mark: bottom right of the footer strip, x 1150 to 1250 px, white, no more than 4 percent of the frame width.

The profile grid centre-crops 4:5 to a square (y 169 to 1519 px), so the headline and the supporting line sit where they survive the grid view. The footer shows in the feed, and the caption repeats the same details, so the grid view is covered either way.

### Template B: Reel

Spec: 9:16, 1080 x 1920 px.

Layout zones:

- Headline zone: y 240 to 540 px, x 80 to 900 px, centred, one to two lines.
- Supporting line: y 560 to 660 px, one line.
- Content zone: y 700 to 1180 px. The product, the build or the screen recording sits here.
- Footer strip: y 1240 to 1540 px, a full-width #0f131f band, text inside x 80 to 900 px. Same order as Template A: price, marketplaces (Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho), city (New Delhi), WhatsApp +91 75575 75514.
- Logo mark: bottom right of the footer strip, x 780 to 900 px, white, small.

Keep-out zones on a Reel, where Instagram puts its own interface: the top 170 px, the right 160 px column and the bottom 360 px. No text, logo or essential graphic may sit inside them.

## 5. Typography and spacing

- One heading weight: a heavy or bold weight, 700 or 800. One body weight: regular or medium, 400 or 500. Two weights, one family, no mixing.
- Minimum readable sizes on a phone screen:
  - Headline: 90 px minimum at 1350 px width, 96 px minimum at 1080 px width. Never below 72 px anywhere.
  - Supporting line: 54 to 64 px.
  - Footer line: 48 px minimum.
  - No information-carrying text below 44 px.
- Avoid thin and light weights (100 to 300) on #07090e. They disappear on poor screens.
- Line spacing: 1.2 to 1.35 for headlines, 1.4 to 1.5 for body.
- Safe margins, already built into the zones above: static 100 px on the sides and 120 px at the top; Reel 170 px at the top, 160 px on the right, 360 px at the bottom. No text or logo outside these margins, so nothing collides with the Instagram interface.

## 6. Accessibility

- Burn captions into every Reel. Most people watch muted, so every spoken or implied line appears as on-screen text.
- High contrast: white text on #07090e or #0f131f. Where text sits over an image, put a #0f131f panel or a 70 percent dark scrim behind it so the text never fights the picture.
- Essential information - price, marketplaces, city, WhatsApp number - appears in the frame, not only in the caption.
- No thin light-weight fonts on the dark background.
- Test: view the asset at half brightness on a cheap phone in daylight. If any text is hard to read, thicken the weight or raise the scrim.

## 7. Pre-publish checklist

Run this before posting anything:

- [ ] Aspect ratio correct: 1350 x 1688 (4:5) for static and carousel, 1080 x 1920 (9:16) for Reels
- [ ] Price present in the frame and in the caption, from Rs 249
- [ ] WhatsApp number +91 75575 75514 present in the frame and in the caption
- [ ] City present: New Delhi
- [ ] Marketplaces present: Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho
- [ ] Caption burned into every Reel
- [ ] Contrast legible: white on dark, no thin weights, readable at thumbnail size
- [ ] No text inside the Instagram interface zones
- [ ] No claim, in copy or placeholder text, that the product was physically shot or that the output is a real photograph
- [ ] No camera or studio stock imagery in the asset
- [ ] Any product shown keeps its real shape, colour and label text

## Honesty rules

The method: the set, the lighting and the background are generated around the client's real product photos. Sudhir does not physically shoot.

- No template may include placeholder copy that implies a conventional camera studio. Nothing about shoots, shoot days, cameras, crews or a studio session.
- No sample text may claim the output is a real photograph.
- No stock photography of a camera or a studio anywhere, because it would misrepresent how the work is made.
- Keep the product layer untouched: same shape, same colour, same label text.

Approved phrasing:

| Do not write | Write |
|---|---|
| We photographed your product | Set, lighting and background built around your product photos |
| Real studio photograph | Studio style images made from your own product photos |
| Shot in our Delhi studio | Built in New Delhi around your real photos |

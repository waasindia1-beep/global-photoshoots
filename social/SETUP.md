# Getting found: Instagram, Facebook and Google

Global Photoshoots, New Delhi. Product photography and short product video, INR 249 to 1299 a project, delivery in 24 to 48 hours, every file belongs to the client.

Two things to hold on to while you work through this:

- You build the set, the lighting and the background around the client's real product photos. Never describe the output as a photograph you took. Buyers work that out, and a seller who feels misled does not come back.
- Nobody searches for "Sudhir Kumar Solanki". They search for the service and the city. This page is about being findable, not about being clever.

## 1. Instagram display name

Change it from `Sudhir Kumar Solanki` to:

```
Product Photography | Delhi
```

That is 27 characters and the field allows 30.

The display name is the only field Instagram reads when somebody searches, and it matches on service and place words. With your own name sitting there you are invisible to anyone typing "product photography Delhi", which is the only search that matters here. Keep your name in your professional field or in the bio instead, so the person who is told your name can still reach you.

If you would rather drop the pipe, `Product Photography Delhi` at 25 characters works just as well. Do not put your name back in this field.

## 2. Instagram category label

Switch the account to a Business or Creator account, then in the category picker choose **Photographer**.

The category does not affect Instagram search. It is used for suggestions in the Nearby tab, so set it once and leave it. If your picker does not offer Photographer, use Digital Creator as the second choice.

## 4. Instagram Highlights

Create them in this order and set each cover before you add the rest. Covers should be plain, one word, readable at thumbnail size on a phone.

| Order | Cover concept | What goes inside |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | How It Works | 5 slides, text on plain backgrounds. 1: send your product photos on WhatsApp. 2: we build the set, the light and the background. 3: first preview back the same day. 4: final files in 24 to 48 hours. 5: every file is yours, no watermark, no monthly fee |
| 2 | Pricing | Your four tiers at 249, 499, 799 and 1299, with what each includes: number of images, sizes, formats, how many revisions. Finish on: price confirmed on WhatsApp before we start |
| 3 | Amazon and Flipkart Specs | Pure white main image at 1600 px and above, product filling about 85% of the frame, no props, no text, no extra logos on the image. Then the Myntra and Meesho sizes. Add one line that you deliver to spec, because that is the question sellers actually ask |
| 4 | Before / After | Your strongest asset. Same product, their phone photo on the left, the finished image on the right. 5 to 8 pairs, one per slide. Spend the time here, this is the highlight that sells the work |
| 5 | BTS | 6 slides maximum. Screen recordings of the build, the set going up, the lighting, the file handover. Buyers like seeing the machine, but do not turn it into a tutorial |
| 6 | Reviews | **Add later, once you have any.** See the note underneath |

On Reviews: do not publish an empty highlight. A cover that says Reviews with nothing behind it reads worse than no cover at all. Ask five past clients on WhatsApp for one line about the work, screenshot it with the name and number blurred, and put those in. When you have them, create the cover last. If after two weeks you cannot get a single screenshot, skip this highlight and take it off the grid.

## 5. Instagram pinned posts

Exactly three, in this order. A visitor who lands on the profile reads these and then decides. Everything else on the grid is supporting material.

| Pin | What it must contain |
|---|---|
| 1. Your story | Who you are, that you work in New Delhi, and how the work is done. One image with text on it, or a 3 slide carousel. Say plainly that you build the set, the lighting and the background around the client's own product photos, and that the client keeps every file. Finish on the WhatsApp number. This is the post that kills the "is this a toy" worry before it is spoken |
| 2. Services and pricing | The four tiers with what each includes and the delivery time. One clean price card works, or a carousel with a tier per slide. Sellers compare prices before they compare looks, so do not hide the numbers |
| 3. Most booked offer, or social proof | You have no reviews yet, so pin whichever offer gets ordered most. A before and after of that offer, with the image count and the turnaround on the slide. The moment you have a client screenshot, drop it into this same post so the pin keeps working without you touching it again |

Grid rule for the first nine posts: alternate the type so the grid does not look flat. One finished image, one before and after, one BTS, one price card, one shot of the set, and repeat. Nine near identical product shots tell a visitor nothing.

Facebook is not where Delhi buyers look for a photographer, so this page exists for name consistency and for the person who types your name into Facebook after seeing it on Instagram. That is the entire return, so size the effort to match.

Rules for this page:

## 7. Where the effort should go instead: Google Business Profile

This is the one that pays. You have no Google Business Profile and you do not appear in Maps for "product photography New Delhi". Lohar Studio carries 638 plus Google reviews on that same search. In this trade the trust lives on the map pin, not on Instagram. A Maps listing with reviews beats a polished Instagram with none, every time.

**Set it up this week, 2 to 3 hours.**

1. Create a Google Business Profile at google.com/business. It is free.
2. Business name: Global Photoshoots. Do not bolt keywords, the city or the word AI onto the name. It has to match every other listing you have.
3. Category: Photographer. Then add every extra category the picker offers that genuinely fits, such as Product photography service or Video production service if they are listed.
4. Address: settle this question first. A service area business working from home can show its area without showing the address, which keeps your home address off a public map. If you would rather show an address to look established, use 5B/23 Vishnu Garden, New Delhi 110018. Both are legitimate, but choose on purpose rather than by accident.
5. Hours: the hours you genuinely answer WhatsApp, not the hours you wish you did.
6. Links: your website, plus a WhatsApp link using wa.me/917557575514.
7. Services list: product photography, Amazon listing images, Flipkart listing images, catalogue photography, lifestyle set, short product video.
8. Photos: at least 10, uploaded on day one. Three before and after pairs, four finished images, two of the set or the process, your logo.
9. Description, up to 750 characters, written as plain text with the words buyers actually type: "Product photography New Delhi. Amazon and Flipkart listing images. Myntra and Meesho catalogue sets. Studio set, lighting and background built around your real product photos. 24 to 48 hour delivery. Every file belongs to you. WhatsApp 75575 75514."

**Then the part that actually moves the pin: reviews.**

- Ask every client, every time, on WhatsApp. Line to send: "If the work was useful, a one line Google review helps me a lot. Here is the link." Then send the listing link straight after it.
- Reply to every review, good or bad, within a week. Google reads the replies and so do buyers.
- Target 20 reviews in 90 days. At your volume that is one a week or better.
- Never pay for reviews, never write one for a client, never ask someone with no project to review. That is the fastest way to get a profile closed.
- The reviewer is the client, and the client really did receive the files, so this is honest. Attaching a finished image as the review photo is fine.

**Keep it alive.** One new photo a week, one Google post a month, one price update a month. Fix anything that goes stale, especially the hours.

**Do not run Google Ads yet.** Spend nothing until you have 15 or 20 reviews, because paid clicks into a profile with three reviews waste the money.

**Link the two together.** Put the Google listing link in your Instagram bio, in the website field or on your website. Put your Instagram handle in the Google description. They feed each other, and Instagram is the shop window for Google.

Where the time actually goes: Instagram about 2 hours in the first week then 15 minutes a week, Google Business Profile 3 hours to set up then 30 minutes a week, Facebook 90 minutes once.

## 8. How to describe the work

The one rule that matters. You generate the set, the lighting and the background around the client's real product photos. You have not held a camera.

| Do not say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| We photographed your product | We built the set, lighting and background around your product photos |
| Real studio photograph | Studio style images made from your own product photos |
| AI shoot | Generated set and lighting, your product unchanged |
| We shot it at your place | Send us your photos on WhatsApp and we do the rest |

Keep the product exactly as it arrived. Same shape, same logo, same colour, same label. Do not redesign the packaging or invent a finish that is not there, that is how a seller ends up with a problem from Amazon later.

If a client or a marketplace asks whether the images were generated, say yes, straight away. You will say it anyway, and being the one who says it first is worth more than the images are. Sellers who already knew tend to stay.

HOW TO ADD YOUR OWN PHOTOS & AI RENDERS:
=========================================
1. Simply copy your image files (.jpg or .png) into this folder.
2. In `index.html`, find the <img> tags under the <section id="portfolio">.
3. Replace the Unsplash URLs with your local relative path, for example:
   `src="assets/my-fashion-render.jpg"`
4. Save index.html and refresh your browser!

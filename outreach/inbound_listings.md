# Inbound Listings Pack — where the calls come from

Outreach finds you clients. These listings are what make **strangers find you** when
they search for the service. This is the difference between one good week and a
steadier trickle, and for a service business in India it is usually the larger half.

Work top to bottom. Google first — it feeds everything else.

---

## ⚠️ Before you start: two things that block everything

1. **Your real business address.** `config.js` currently says
   `REPLACE WITH YOUR REAL ADDRESS`. Google, Justdial and IndiaMART all verify it, and
   they cross-check it against each other. An address that does not match across
   listings is the most common reason these get suspended.
2. **Answer the phone.** Your number **+91 75575 75514** is your call line. A WhatsApp
   Business voice call and a normal mobile call both land on it. Google will rank you
   lower and people will not call twice if you miss the first five calls. Pick your
   hours and answer.

## 1. Google Business Profile — do this first

Free, and it is the single highest-return item in this document. It is what appears
when someone searches *"product photography [your city]"*, and it produces actual phone
calls.

Go to `business.google.com` → **Add a business**. Use the **free** tier, not the paid
one, to start.

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| Business name | `Global Photoshoots` |
| Category (primary) | `Photographer` |
| Category 2 | `Commercial Photography` / `Product Photography` if offered |
| Address | Your real address (must match your other listings) |
| Phone | `+91 75575 75514` — use this as the primary click-to-call number |
| Website | `https://global-photoshoots.vercel.app/` |
| Hours | Set honest hours. Include a Saturday slot — that is when small brands reply. |
| Description | Copy from section 2 below |

**Then, the part everyone skips — verification and photos.** An unverified profile
does not rank and does not get calls.

- Verify by postcard or video (video is faster)
- Upload **at least 9 photos**. Do not skip this. Businesses with photos get
  meaningfully more direction requests.
- Use your **real** portfolio images, not the stock placeholders. This is the reason
  the portfolio swap matters: it is not just about the website any more.
- Add your services with prices, so you show up in price-filtered searches:
  Product Photography · E-commerce Product Images · Amazon Listing Photography ·
  Fashion Lookbook · Beauty Product Photography · Jewellery Macro Photography ·
  AI Product Photography · Product Video

**Weekly habit:** post one photo. A profile that posts is a profile that ranks. This is
ten minutes a week and it compounds.

## 4. IndiaMART — for B2B enquiry flow

IndiaMART behaves less like a directory and more like an inbox: buyers send you
enquiries through the platform. That suits you because a real buyer arrives with a
requirement already written.

- Register as a **seller** under *Media & Advertising → Photography & Videography*
- List as a **supplier**, not a service provider, if both options appear
- Add `product photography`, `ecommerce photography`, `amazon listing images`,
  `fashion catalogue` as keywords
- Upload the same three tiers as individual product listings with images and prices.
  IndiaMART buyers search by product, so listings beat a description.
- Respond to enquiries **within an hour**. Response time is the single biggest ranking
  factor on that platform.

## 5. The questions these listings will produce

Have answers ready for the three that come up every time:

**"Is this AI or real photography?"**
Say what your process actually is. Do not claim real photography if it is not. What you
can say honestly and still sell with: you deliver full commercial copyright, you deliver
in 24-48 hours, and you will produce a free sample so they can judge the output before
paying anything.

**"Do you shoot the actual physical product?"**
Answer this precisely. It matters for jewellery, watches and anything where dimensions
and materials have to be true. If you need their product reference photos for an accurate
render, say so — it sets a clear expectation and prevents a dispute later.

**"Can we own the images outright?"**
Yes, and that is your strongest technical argument against every cheaper option. Put it
in the first paragraph of every listing and every email.

## 7. Tracked links — know which channel earns

The site records where every enquiry came from and prints it in the brief you receive
(`Reference: gbp` and so on). Use a **different tagged link per channel**, otherwise you
will never know whether Justdial or Instagram is paying for itself.

| Channel | Link to use |
| --- | --- |
| Google Business Profile | `https://global-photoshoots.vercel.app/?src=gbp` |
| Justdial | `https://global-photoshoots.vercel.app/?src=justdial` |
| IndiaMART | `https://global-photoshoots.vercel.app/?src=indiamart` |
| Instagram bio | `https://global-photoshoots.vercel.app/?src=instagram` |
| Cold WhatsApp messages | `https://global-photoshoots.vercel.app/?src=wa-outreach` |
| Cold email | `https://global-photoshoots.vercel.app/?src=email` |
| Referral / word of mouth | `https://global-photoshoots.vercel.app/?src=referral` |

`utm_source` works too if you prefer standard analytics naming. Anything unrecognised
falls back to `direct`.

After two weeks, sort your brief inbox by `Reference:` and you have the answer to the
only question that matters — **where should the next hour go?**

## 8. What to check each week

Not a suggestion — the thing that decides whether this works:

- [ ] Any new calls or enquiries? Which listing did they come from?
- [ ] Google: is the profile showing the review prompt to customers you just delivered?
- [ ] Have you posted one photo to Google?
- [ ] IndiaMART: respond to every enquiry within the hour
- [ ] Are you asking every completed customer for a Google review?

**Ask for reviews.** A profile with 15 reviews outranks one with 3, and reviews are the
cheapest conversion lever available to you. Send the link right after handover, while they
are happy.
